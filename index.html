ABOUT MONEYMIND

A focused workflow for short-form content.

MoneyMind is a creator-focused workflow application designed to help organize product-video planning, review, and publishing activities. TikTok access is used only after a creator authorizes the application and only for enabled features and permissions.

01

Plan

Prepare product-focused video concepts, hooks, captions, and calls to action.

02

Create & Review

Review the content package and video asset before any publishing action is taken.

03

Publish

Use creator-authorized TikTok capabilities to upload or publish approved content.

Your account stays under your control.

MoneyMind does not publish content to TikTok without the creator's authorization. Access can be revoked through the applicable TikTok account controls.
